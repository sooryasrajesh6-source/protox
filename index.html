ABOUT PROTOX

Build. Simulate. Understand.

A practical one-day workshop covering two essential tools in modern electronics: PCB Design and LTspice circuit simulation.

TRACK 01

PCB Design

Understand the journey from schematic to a professional printed circuit board layout.

  • Schematic and component planning
  • PCB layout fundamentals
  • Routing and design considerations
  • Practical design workflow
TRACK 02

LTspice Simulation

Explore circuit simulation and learn how to test circuit behavior before hardware implementation.

  • Introduction to circuit simulation
  • SPICE fundamentals
  • Waveform and circuit analysis
  • Practical simulation workflow
